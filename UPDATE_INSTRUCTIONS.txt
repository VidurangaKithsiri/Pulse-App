Close Android Studio. Extract this ZIP into your EXISTING project root (the folder containing app and gradlew.bat). Choose Replace for matching files. Keep all other project files.
Run 1_SETUP_GUARDIAN_BACKEND.cmd again to update Firebase. Then open your existing Android project, Sync Gradle and Run.
